KENYA STREET SIM — DROP REAL 3D TRAFFIC VEHICLES HERE
======================================================

Drop any commercial-safe / Unity Asset Store / Blender / CC0 3D vehicle models into this folder:
  Supported formats: .glb, .gltf, .fbx, .obj, .prefab

Then in Unity 6, open:
  Menu -> Kenya Street Sim -> Commercial Ownership & Traffic Manager -> "Rebuild 16-Town Kenya Map"

Every vehicle you drop here is AUTOMATICALLY:
  1. Oriented forward (+Z) and scaled to realistic road length (cars ~4.6m, vans/SUVs ~5.0m, trucks ~7.8m, buses ~11.8m)
  2. Grounded cleanly onto the Kenyan tarmac (Y = 0)
  3. Fitted with spinning wheels + respectful zero-push Left-Hand Traffic AI (pulls over for Police sirens!)
  4. Spawned across major Kenyan towns (Nairobi, Nakuru, Kisumu, Eldoret, Mombasa) and open highways!

TIP FOR COMMERCIAL RELEASE (Path A):
  Only drop models you made yourself in Blender, bought on the Unity Asset Store / Fab / CGTrader
  with a commercial license, or downloaded under CC0 / CC BY 4.0 (Commercial).

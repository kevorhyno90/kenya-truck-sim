# Kenya Street Sim — Savanna Line: Road to the Coast 🇰🇪

A standalone commercial **Unity 6 (URP) + Blender 5.3** Kenyan Bus & Logistics Simulator inspired by *Euro Truck Simulator 2* & *American Truck Simulator*, spanning **41 authentic Kenyan towns**, **15 interconnected highways**, **10 multi-exit roundabouts**, and real 3D Rift Valley & Highland terrain.

---

## 🌍 41-Town National Kenya Highway Map

- **South Nyanza & Lake Victoria (A1 / C20 / C19 / C18):** Migori/Sirare Tanzania Border → Awendo (Sugarcane Belt) → Rongo → Rodi Kopany (→ Ndhiwa → Sori) → Homa Bay → Kendu Bay → Suneka → Kisii → Oyugis → Sondu → Katito → Ahero → Kisumu City (Intl Airport & Kibuye Market).
- **Western & Uganda Border (A1 / C33 / A104):** Kisumu → Vihiga → Kakamega (Equatorial Rainforest) → Webuye → Kiminini → Kitale → North, plus Kisumu → Bungoma → Malaba Uganda Border.
- **Highlands Tea Belt & Rift Valley (B3 / B1 / C25 / C88 / A104):** Kisii → Keroka → Chebilat (→ Sondu) → Sotik → Kaplong → Bomet → Narok (Wildlife Crossing) → Mai Mahiu (Rift Escarpment) → Naivasha / Nairobi, plus Kaplong → Kericho (Emerald Tea Estates) → Total (Mau Summit) → Salgaa → Nakuru → Timboroa Forest → Eldoret Intl Airport.
- **Capital, Thika & Indian Ocean Coast (A2 / A109 / B8):** Nairobi CBD (6-lane & JKIA) → Thika 6-Lane Superhighway, plus Nairobi → Mtito Andei → Voi / Tsavo (Red-Earth Baobabs & Elephants) → Mombasa (Moi Avenue Tusks & Moi Intl Airport) → Kilifi → Malindi Coast.

## 🚌 Bus Business Story Mode — *Savanna Line: Road to the Coast*

- **Chapter 1 — The Migori Hustle:** Start in Migori driving a hired 14-seater Matatu (30% daily owner *hesabu* cut).
- **Chapter 2 — Your First Coach & SACCO:** Buy your own vehicle (keep 100% fares), name your Kenyan SACCO, and hire a *Makanga* Conductor (`+35%` fare & parcel tips + live Swahili/English boarding calls).
- **Chapter 3 — Night Express & Rivals:** Beat rival SACCO timetables for cash bonuses, hire AI Route Drivers across 4 corridors for passive income, pay real diesel costs, service tires/brakes, and take cross-border parcel contracts to **Migori/Sirare & Malaba Uganda**.
- **Chapter 4 — National Fleet Empire:** Expand across all 41 towns and lead the flagship run to **Mombasa, Kilifi & Malindi**.
